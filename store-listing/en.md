# Shendu

Select Chinese text, right-click, and view contextual pinyin offline. Use the speaker button to hear the text with your system Chinese voice.

Shendu only receives temporary access after you invoke its menu. It has no account, ads, analytics, network requests, or saved browsing data.

Unsure how to pronounce Chinese text on a webpage? Select it, right-click and choose “Show Chinese pinyin”. A small popover near the selection shows the Chinese text and tone-marked pinyin. Press the speaker button to hear the displayed Chinese target using a local Chinese voice on your device.

Shendu supports simplified and traditional Chinese, displaying up to the first eight Han characters in a selection. It uses up to 32 surrounding characters on each side to help identify common polyphonic words such as 银行, 重庆 and 音乐. Surrounding context is not included in the spoken text. Pronunciation inference is not guaranteed, and a system voice may pronounce an isolated polyphonic character differently from the displayed pinyin.

Pinyin is computed locally. Speech uses only Chinese voices identified by the browser as local services. If none is available, pinyin still works and the popover explains why speech is unavailable. No sign-up or server configuration is needed.

Designed for ordinary HTTP/HTTPS webpage text. Input fields, rich-text and online document editors, browser PDF viewers, image OCR and internal browser pages are outside the supported scope. Click outside, press Escape, scroll, or wait for the popover to close automatically.

Permissions are limited to creating the context menu and temporarily accessing the current page after your action to display the popover. Shendu does not request permanent website access or store browsing history or selected text.
